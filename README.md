# Light & Shape · 光与形

An interactive, bilingual (English / 中文) study of the art direction, visual philosophy and story of Bungie's **Destiny 2**, across every expansion from The Red War to Renegades.

一份可互动的中英双语研究：Bungie《命运2》的美术方向、视觉哲学与故事，覆盖从红色战争到 Renegades 的全部资料片。

- Pure static site (HTML/CSS/JS), no build step. Open `index.html` or host on GitHub Pages.
- Gallery: 98 pieces of Destiny 2 concept art (`art.js`), each credited to its Bungie artist and loaded directly from the artist's public ArtStation post, with a bilingual reading of how it is designed and how it echoes the story.
- Soundtrack: official Bungie / Destiny 2 YouTube uploads, embedded in a mini player that keeps playing while you scroll.
- Diagrams (hero, playgrounds, faction glyphs) are original SVG.
- Keyboard: `←` `→` move between sections (inside the Gallery they step through expansions; in the image viewer they step through images), `Esc` closes the viewer, `L` toggles language.
