# Light & Shape · 光与形

An interactive, bilingual (English / 中文) study of the art direction, visual philosophy and story of Bungie's **Destiny 2**, across every expansion, season and episode from The Red War to Renegades.

一份可互动的中英双语研究：Bungie《命运2》的美术方向、视觉哲学与故事，覆盖从《猩红战争》到《反叛》的全部资料片、赛季与篇章。

- Pure static site (HTML/CSS/JS), no build step. Open `index.html` or host on GitHub Pages.
- Gallery: 171 pieces of Destiny 2 concept art in 14 chapters (`art.js`, extended by `art2.js`), shown in a two-row horizontal scroller, each credited to its Bungie artist and loaded directly from the artist's public ArtStation post, with a bilingual reading of how it is designed and how it echoes the story.
- Soundtrack: official Bungie / Destiny 2 YouTube uploads, embedded in a mini player that keeps playing while you scroll.
- Chinese names follow the official Simplified Chinese localisation (Steam store and Bungie's zh-chs game database).
- Diagrams (hero, playgrounds, faction glyphs) are original SVG.
- Keyboard: `←` `→` move between sections (inside the Gallery they step through expansions; in the image viewer they step through images), `Esc` closes the viewer, `L` toggles language.
